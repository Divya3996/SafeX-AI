SafeX AI privacy policy

Version 1.1.0 · 10 October 2026

Local checks

SafeX AI analyzes submitted text, URLs, permitted visible page content, form metadata and deliberately chosen images on your computer. It does not upload scan inputs, screenshots, findings or feedback. All model, OCR and QR runtime files are packaged. The extension makes no remote reputation queries and has no analytics or account system.

Site access

Manual page checks use temporary access granted by your interaction with the extension. Automatic page checks require optional access to the site you choose. Automatic extraction excludes input values, editable fields, cookies and website storage. For recognized webmail/chat sites, automatic text extraction is disabled; select or paste the message you want reviewed. Scanning cannot verify sender identity or account authenticity.

Images

Capture temporarily holds the visible active tab locally. Review the preview and choose a crop or cancel. The full frame is discarded after you accept the crop. Only the chosen image is recognized. Crops and recognized text are not saved in scan history. Screenshots and voluntarily submitted content may contain sensitive information.

Storage

Preferences, introduction/tour completion, imported threat lists, deliberately saved redacted reports and local feedback are stored in your browser profile. Temporary result data expires after 15 minutes and is periodically removed. Saved reports remove original messages, screenshot pixels, payment identity, URL credentials, query values and fragments. Retention is 7, 30 or 90 days, with a maximum of 200 saved reports. You can delete reports and imported lists. Chrome storage is not an encrypted vault. Uninstalling the extension removes its Chrome storage.

Blocking

Blocking is disabled by default. If enabled, only exact HTTP(S) addresses that you explicitly label “block” in an imported list are blocked. Reported entries warn but do not independently block. Imported lists expire after 7 days. These are user-managed lists; SafeX does not certify that every listed destination is malicious. Model warnings do not create blocking rules.

Sharing

Exports contain redacted reports and occur only when you choose Export. Feedback remains on this device and is not silently used to train models. Incognito support is disabled. Visiting an external website and Chrome's own extension-update activity are separate from SafeX analysis.

Scam help

Incident choices and checklist ticks remain in this panel's memory. Copying a helpline number requires your click. Calls and official reporting websites open only after your action; the extension does not call anyone or submit complaints automatically.

Limitations and distribution

The extension can miss scams or warn about legitimate requests. It cannot assess inaccessible page content, every redirect, every navigation method or unseen communications. Current models have limited research coverage. Independent browser accuracy has not been established. This local development release is prepared for unpacked installation; a public Web Store listing must supply the publisher identity and support contact before submission.
